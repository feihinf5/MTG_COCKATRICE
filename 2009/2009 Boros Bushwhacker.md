Note
- The Finals09 winning deck by Yamamoto Akimasa
- Turn 1, play an [**Arid Mesa**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/16c8d2fa-54a7-46e8-980c-905258497c90?format=image) (or any red/white Fetchland), crack it to search up a [**Plains**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cc084e24-22fe-4326-b894-54d79fbeba2f?format=image), cast [**Steppe Lynx**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0aaec1c-8084-4468-82e7-2e4cc5ebe244?format=image) (0/1)
- Turn 2, play a [**Mountain**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ed783a0c-ba7c-48f4-aac6-ad93a02bce6b?format=image), [**Steppe Lynx**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0aaec1c-8084-4468-82e7-2e4cc5ebe244?format=image) gets +2/+2 from Landfall (now a 2/3), swing with [**Steppe Lynx**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0aaec1c-8084-4468-82e7-2e4cc5ebe244?format=image) for 2 damage (Opponent at 18), cast [**Plated Geopede**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fd8b5c0c-acda-411e-9f17-6d9292628a56?format=image) (1/1)
- Turn 3, play a second [**Arid Mesa**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/16c8d2fa-54a7-46e8-980c-905258497c90?format=image), crack [**Arid Mesa**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/16c8d2fa-54a7-46e8-980c-905258497c90?format=image) to fetch a [**Mountain**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ed783a0c-ba7c-48f4-aac6-ad93a02bce6b?format=image), second Landfall triggers on both creatures ([**Steppe Lynx**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0aaec1c-8084-4468-82e7-2e4cc5ebe244?format=image) becomes 4/5, [**Plated Geopede**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fd8b5c0c-acda-411e-9f17-6d9292628a56?format=image) becomes 5/5), cast [**Goblin Bushwhacker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4085a5bf-a71b-4c73-9b39-0dcc328fe11b?format=image) with Kicker paid, attack with [**Steppe Lynx**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0aaec1c-8084-4468-82e7-2e4cc5ebe244?format=image) (5/5), [**Plated Geopede**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fd8b5c0c-acda-411e-9f17-6d9292628a56?format=image) (6/5), [**Goblin Bushwhacker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4085a5bf-a71b-4c73-9b39-0dcc328fe11b?format=image) (2/1) for total of 13 damage
