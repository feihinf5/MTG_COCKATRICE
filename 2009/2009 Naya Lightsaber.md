Note
- World Championships 2009 winning deck by Andre Coimbra
- Played enough terrifying early threats ([**Wild Nacatl**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5eb18038-eb6a-4d27-ba52-52a1cee6b512?format=image), [**Woolly Thoctar**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7d5907d5-ae5c-4c9d-a5df-61f1c94f979d?format=image)), the opponent would run out of removal spells, leaving the coast completely clear for [**Baneslayer Angel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6764ea7b-ccb3-4f39-b8ba-654a186210b9?format=image) to single-handedly win the game
- [**Bloodbraid Elf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ef67487-c8e5-49bb-b0f7-e073ff2e31f1?format=image) into [**Woolly Thoctar**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7d5907d5-ae5c-4c9d-a5df-61f1c94f979d?format=image), hitting a 5/4 or a removal spell off a Cascade trigger created an unmatchable tempo swing on turn 3 or 4
- [**Ranger of Eos**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1a30ee26-5f78-4ac2-9105-1baa9ece8a21?format=image) acted as a mid-to-late-game engine that tutored up your 1-drops to immediately rebuild after a board wipe
- [**Ranger of Eos**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1a30ee26-5f78-4ac2-9105-1baa9ece8a21?format=image) could fetch [**Scute Mob**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/43e2fcc6-96d3-45f5-b4dd-e049d9ec6cec?format=image), once you hit 5 lands
- [**Ajani Vengeant**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8fc108a3-15e5-44ab-a6e2-182b309f9c0c?format=image) +1 ability could keep a key land (like a Jund player's only black source) tapped down permanently, while its -2 offered removal and critical life gain to stay ahead against burn and aggro
