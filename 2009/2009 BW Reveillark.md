Note
- 2009 Great Britain National Championship winning deck by Dan Gardner
- [**Knight of the White Orchid**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/642c6354-4dab-45c9-a1a6-57bd16f16e30?format=image) + [**Fieldmist Borderpost**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9650a717-b1e4-4cd2-894d-766f6b311369?format=image), by bouncing a basic land to cast a [**Fieldmist Borderpost**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9650a717-b1e4-4cd2-894d-766f6b311369?format=image) on turn 2, you could guarantee being behind on land count on turn 3—allowing [**Knight of the White Orchid**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/642c6354-4dab-45c9-a1a6-57bd16f16e30?format=image) to search up another Plains and ramp you ahead
- [**Reveillark**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5629edd4-b5d3-43f8-8c7c-f9d916e35158?format=image) could return almost every utility creature in the deck when it left the battlefield—including [**Knight of the White Orchid**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/642c6354-4dab-45c9-a1a6-57bd16f16e30?format=image), [**Mulldrifter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a97cfefa-ade7-49f6-b2aa-1118b9db4935?format=image), [**Meddling Mage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1a55308b-5772-430a-93e4-b65c416b86cf?format=image) and [**Vendilion Clique**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f53d8540-fb6d-4d4c-b467-ebfbfa53c880?format=image) (since all have power 2 or less)
- [**Baneslayer Angel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6764ea7b-ccb3-4f39-b8ba-654a186210b9?format=image) became the undisputed king of finisher threats
