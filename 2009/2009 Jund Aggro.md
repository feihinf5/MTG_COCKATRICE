Note
- 2009 Argentina National Championship winning deck by Alejo Zagalsky
- Favored the immediate, hasty pressure of [**Boggart Ram-Gang**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/63e25b70-fa21-4663-aabd-34b7e0b75c34?format=image) to rush down control players before they could stabilize
- [**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3926f037-aaec-416e-a486-b59aae6faf44?format=image), draws you a card at the end of the turn if an opponent lost 3 or more life via [**Lightning Bolt**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/435589bb-27c6-4a6d-9d63-394d5092b9d8?format=image) (3 damage), [**Blightning**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3c05e8a2-b7d0-4f24-b2ae-8e4db30e5842?format=image) (3 damage), [**Bloodbraid Elf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ef67487-c8e5-49bb-b0f7-e073ff2e31f1?format=image) (3 power with haste),[**Putrid Leech**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aaa47568-5668-4a9f-ad1c-9a13010ffc2b?format=image) (pumps to a 4/4)
- [**Great Sable Stag**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/451c8a79-1e8a-437d-836f-31155914c551?format=image) being uncounterable and having protection from Blue and Black meant that Faeries and Cruel Control literally could not interact with it
- Tribal lands ([**Auntie's Hovel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/098685c9-cd85-4279-a3b5-b495485bba35?format=image), [**Gilt-Leaf Palace**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cc4bbbb5-4218-4b2a-9ca2-de4d5f5dda19?format=image)) and Filter lands ([**Graven Cairns**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/81e7d329-ef6a-45f3-82b6-37a3606c00bc?format=image), [**Fire-Lit Thicket**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0ab9d6ad-f819-4a1e-b4ff-8dc00791f0fd?format=image)) to reliably cast wildly restrictive mana costs
