Note
- 2010 Legacy Championship winning deck by Ryan Messick
- [**Wort, Boggart Auntie**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a08a1377-dede-47c8-8447-c9df125f3b14?format=image) acts as an un-counterable recursive engine when brought out via [**Aether Vial**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/741c479b-5e92-4837-9673-9bc72aa11d26?format=image) or [**Goblin Lackey**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9b848caa-aad8-4060-8f86-304a8556de2d?format=image), returning destroyed Goblins or cycle-cards like [**Gempalm Incinerator**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2687c311-fd0c-4fe0-bce8-e3f412216796?format=image) back to hand every turn
- [**Auntie's Hovel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/098685c9-cd85-4279-a3b5-b495485bba35?format=image) & [**Badlands**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/717f6d10-9144-4ade-9ac6-a481cc66b875?format=image) that enables black mana without needing to compromise tribal synergy or land speed
- [**Warren Weirding**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b0c1288b-5515-497f-b15d-520a29322f5b?format=image) gives the deck maindeckable edict removal that specifically bypasses Protection from Red while still being a searchable target via [**Goblin Matron**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e9e2e5d-ad06-4378-9afb-ffb174e6a5b4?format=image)
- [**Warren Instigator**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/98637219-40d8-414c-939d-e2c90b909725?format=image) has Double Strike, dealing combat damage lets you cheat two Goblins directly onto the battlefield in a single turn (e.g., dropping [**Siege-Gang Commander**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92e78cec-aaf9-4fe8-887b-b7e356d63315?format=image) + [**Goblin Ringleader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6b2cd77-9552-48b1-80cb-26966323c1ea?format=image))
- [**Goblin Matron**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e9e2e5d-ad06-4378-9afb-ffb174e6a5b4?format=image) acts as a tutor for situational silver bullets, [**Stingscourger**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4b10d6b3-2cd9-4628-a079-6ac803b6f3c3?format=image) (bounce huge threats), [**Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/162018eb-5483-4fa2-9c5a-abb639eecf91?format=image) (copy [**Goblin Ringleader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6b2cd77-9552-48b1-80cb-26966323c1ea?format=image)/[**Goblin Matron**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e9e2e5d-ad06-4378-9afb-ffb174e6a5b4?format=image) for infinite value), or [**Siege-Gang Commander**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92e78cec-aaf9-4fe8-887b-b7e356d63315?format=image) (reach/burn)
- [**Goblin Ringleader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6b2cd77-9552-48b1-80cb-26966323c1ea?format=image) combined with [**Aether Vial**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/741c479b-5e92-4837-9673-9bc72aa11d26?format=image) ensures the deck rarely runs out of cards, out-grinding traditional control decks despite being an aggressive creature strategy
