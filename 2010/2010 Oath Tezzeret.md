Note
- 2010 Japan Vintage Championship winning deck by Saitou Yuu
- Uses [**Oath of Druids**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cf14de50-d123-400c-862e-2c95fd2aa23f?format=image) combined with [**Forbidden Orchard**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/88d78261-c8c9-4e0e-b157-f70ed46c3a25?format=image) (which gives the opponent a 1/1 Spirit token) to automatically trigger [**Oath of Druids**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cf14de50-d123-400c-862e-2c95fd2aa23f?format=image) on upkeep and cheat massive threats directly from the deck onto the battlefield for free, [**Emrakul, the Aeons Torn**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/67600383-bbb8-411c-b8e6-2296650bc747?format=image) (15/15 Annihilator) or [**Tidespout Tyrant**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/44865244-2b9f-4734-a4da-49613b23ee4d?format=image) (infinite bounce engine with zero-cost artifact mana)
- Combines [**Time Vault**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/902441dc-c976-4c92-b897-6376eaa0fe38?format=image) + [**Voltaic Key**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1aa4baf7-4693-4c55-af04-2fa5d901d701?format=image) (or [**Tezzeret the Seeker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3b214b6f-4734-4200-8467-92d7e3469b5d?format=image)) to take infinite consecutive turns
- [**Yawgmoth's Will**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6d3e3c3a-d351-4d91-8884-312d4b6f540d?format=image) to re-cast the entire graveyard
