Note
- Pro Tour-San Juan 2010 winning deck by Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa
- Pairing [**Oracle of Mul Daya**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7f89a173-0b2f-4a6a-b706-9aed8dbcabec?format=image) with [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) created an unstoppable card-filtering engine, [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image)'s +2 ability allows you to place extra lands from your hand directly onto the top of your deck, which [**Oracle of Mul Daya**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7f89a173-0b2f-4a6a-b706-9aed8dbcabec?format=image) immediately plays for free
- Ramps up massive pools of mana to either cast [**Comet Storm**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/58d8b2c8-7afe-4299-b59b-8cd2cdc8d4cb?format=image) for multi-target lethal damage or flood the board with [**Avenger of Zendikar**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/dde073e0-f329-4ab0-8e31-a48929e017ce?format=image) and scale plants via fetch lands
- Takes advantage of bounce-land synergies ([**Halimar Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c5651e5e-2314-4e3d-a2c8-2579239314e1?format=image)) and extra land-play effects ([**Oracle of Mul Daya**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7f89a173-0b2f-4a6a-b706-9aed8dbcabec?format=image), [**Explore**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d8f81e8a-e309-4bac-81de-326756cd8901?format=image)) to make [**Deprive**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2efecdd9-bd3a-4b79-92da-6485589d5bde?format=image) operate as a 2-mana hard counter spell with virtually no downside
