Note
- 2010 Block Constructed Championship winning deck by Matt Severa
- Uses [**Khalni Garden**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1cc6a5e6-0b73-4488-8954-4b168ce7106d?format=image), [**Nest Invader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/24517d9c-6cde-41e8-9e82-ee73f069379a?format=image) and [**Kozilek's Predator**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4ab37d0e-5542-4ae0-a48e-d0a4e6bf48e7?format=image) to quickly generate 0/1 Plant and 0/1 Eldrazi Spawn tokens
- Once [**Beastmaster Ascension**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/02d7ea78-d539-4c34-8bb3-941353e3da46?format=image) hits the battlefield, attacking with 7 creatures at once immediately puts 7 counters on it, permanently giving all your creatures +5/+5, turning tiny 0/1 tokens into lethal 5/6 threats in a single combat step
- [**Eldrazi Monument**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aa9f4169-8c49-41f4-9e01-20de06ca4ad9?format=image) serves as a backup to [**Beastmaster Ascension**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/02d7ea78-d539-4c34-8bb3-941353e3da46?format=image), giving the wide token board Indestructible, Flying, and +1/+1, while easily feeding its sacrifice requirement using Eldrazi Spawn or Plant tokens
