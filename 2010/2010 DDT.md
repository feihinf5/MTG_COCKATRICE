Note
- Grand-Prix Houston 2010 winning deck by Adam Yurchick
- [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92409c3a-fb1a-4205-9fe1-0f5affc7b21d?format=image) + [**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/93d2c4d1-6205-404a-b03d-995b90a3a33a?format=image), sacrifice [**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/93d2c4d1-6205-404a-b03d-995b90a3a33a?format=image) to instantly remove all 10 ice counters from [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92409c3a-fb1a-4205-9fe1-0f5affc7b21d?format=image), creating a 20/20 flying, indestructible Marit Lage token as early as Turn 2
- [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/42b8d797-b01d-49cf-9818-d84bba17029d?format=image) + [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e9f13705-6ede-4c29-a2b4-a082bf69e9c5?format=image), sacrifice [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e9f13705-6ede-4c29-a2b4-a082bf69e9c5?format=image) to [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/42b8d797-b01d-49cf-9818-d84bba17029d?format=image) to create a 1/1 flying Thopter and gain 1 life, the 1/1 token triggering [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e9f13705-6ede-4c29-a2b4-a082bf69e9c5?format=image) to return from the graveyard, allowing you to repeat for infinite life and 1/1 fliers with sufficient mana
- [**Muddle the Mixture**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4cc785b0-0a77-4b02-b0b4-2bda2fc621cc?format=image) serves double-duty as a cheap counterspell and a Transmute tutor for 2-mana combo pieces ([**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/93d2c4d1-6205-404a-b03d-995b90a3a33a?format=image), [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/42b8d797-b01d-49cf-9818-d84bba17029d?format=image), [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e9f13705-6ede-4c29-a2b4-a082bf69e9c5?format=image)) or silver-bullet removal ([**Smother**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/09b4deea-c077-46ab-898f-41b3907ecf33?format=image), [**Slaughter Pact**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/42696fdb-de1f-44ae-bef3-b6af068958d0?format=image))
- [**Tolaria West**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a9e80f06-ea05-4151-920f-7ddc88952169?format=image) transmutes for {0} to tutor directly for [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92409c3a-fb1a-4205-9fe1-0f5affc7b21d?format=image), [**Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), [**Academy Ruins**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/af09af65-0a3b-42df-b0fd-372e2158beac?format=image) or [**Engineered Explosives**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8492a272-e595-4f94-a6eb-08d29f211fd6?format=image)
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) provided an inescapable alternative win condition and card-filtering engine
- This exact hybrid shell terrorized the 2009–2010 Extended format, which directly led Wizards of the Coast to ban both [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/92409c3a-fb1a-4205-9fe1-0f5affc7b21d?format=image) and [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e9f13705-6ede-4c29-a2b4-a082bf69e9c5?format=image) when the Modern format was created in 2011.
