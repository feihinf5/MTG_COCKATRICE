Note
- Grand-Prix Houston 2010 winning deck by Adam Yurchick
- [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) + [**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), sacrifice [**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) to instantly remove all 10 ice counters from [**Dark Depths**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), creating a 20/20 flying, indestructible Marit Lage token as early as Turn 2
- [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) + [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), sacrifice [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) to [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) to create a 1/1 flying Thopter and gain 1 life, the 1/1 token triggering [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) to return from the graveyard, allowing you to repeat for infinite life and 1/1 fliers with sufficient mana
- [**Muddle the Mixture**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image) serves double-duty as a cheap counterspell and a Transmute tutor for 2-mana combo pieces ([**Vampire Hexmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image), [**Sword of the Meek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/REPLACE_WITH_APPROPRIATE_UUID?format=image)) or silver-bullet removal ([**Smother**], [**Slaughter Pact**])
- [**Tolaria West**] transmutes for {0} to tutor directly for [**Dark Depths**], [**Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**], [**Academy Ruins**] or [**Engineered Explosives**]
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**] provided an inescapable alternative win condition and card-filtering engine
- This exact hybrid shell terrorized the 2009–2010 Extended format, which directly led Wizards of the Coast to ban both [**Dark Depths**] and [**Sword of the Meek**] when the Modern format was created in 2011.
