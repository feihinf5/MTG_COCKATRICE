Note
- The Finals10 winning deck by Senba Koutarou
- Built around putting 5 quest counters on [**Quest for the Holy Relic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/00d6b8bb-ec11-4ded-a7fc-fa4ea0bb96cc?format=image) to search for [**Argentum Armor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1283c05a-905b-421a-9096-e86b9c807aaf?format=image)
- Uses 0-mana creature drops ([**Memnite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/469cc4e0-49c0-4009-97ea-28e44addec69?format=image), [**Ornithopter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/331a0a01-0c12-4999-9bd7-f26991e4dad5?format=image)) to rapidly trigger [**Quest for the Holy Relic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/00d6b8bb-ec11-4ded-a7fc-fa4ea0bb96cc?format=image) on turns 1 and 2
- Leverages [**Glint Hawk**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/284c4710-4183-4743-9c8b-515cc98cbbb8?format=image) and [**Kor Skyfisher**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/bb2e9465-f5ba-4c7b-9f03-d40dc8394acd?format=image) to bounce 0-mana artifacts or [**Squadron Hawk**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/beddd409-0154-45a5-a20d-833cf1b5e1f4?format=image) back to hand, replaying them to generate multiple Quest counters in a single turn
- [**Stoneforge Mystic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19557351-b65f-4b04-b971-66abdc07000a?format=image) tutors out backup equipment ([**Sword of Body and Mind**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/03cc5caf-b2d7-4211-a1a4-f0ad6e70e3f4?format=image) or [**Argentum Armor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1283c05a-905b-421a-9096-e86b9c807aaf?format=image)
- [**Kor Outfitter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/00006596-1166-4a79-8443-ca9f82e6db4e?format=image) automatically attaches heavy equipment directly onto a creature from the battlefield
