Note
- World Championships 2010 Extended format top deck by Jonathan Randle
- [**Bitterblossom**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8145fed6-6b51-420a-84cf-4ea5e0aa1883?format=image) continuously generates 1/1 flying Faerie tokens, which fuel [**Spellstutter Sprite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4e5ba4a9-a282-4d4b-b25a-179e05e458f4?format=image)'s counter-spell scaling, untap [**Secluded Glen**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e4afa65-7933-4a64-b50f-a9a9f832b112?format=image) and provide fodder for [**Mistbind Clique**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cfc421e2-0dd4-4bdf-b5f5-a60c4b0df63b?format=image)
- Flash-in [**Mistbind Clique**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cfc421e2-0dd4-4bdf-b5f5-a60c4b0df63b?format=image) during the opponent's upkeep to champion a token and tap all their lands, effectively skipping their turn while building a 4/4 flying threat
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) alongside [**Cryptic Command**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/829e3d6e-5d7c-4cc4-a7a6-7cbf5a7442ba?format=image) as the ultimate late-game draw engine
- [**Mutavault**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8ca3c48b-f104-4292-9a4e-2ce87a65893c?format=image) and [**Creeping Tar Pit**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0f427f0b-034c-4821-8758-e395c0042d8a?format=image), making the deck nearly immune to sorcery-speed removal and enabling massive damage pushes out of nowhere
