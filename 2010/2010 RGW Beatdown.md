Note
- 2010 Great Britain National Championship winning deck by Joseph Jackson
- Uses [**Fauna Shaman**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c685e4c3-eb7b-4b9e-9676-395d69d80974?format=image) to pitch creatures (like [**Vengevine**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/51eb9f05-9d5a-4196-9329-626ce4793c42?format=image)) into the graveyard to tutor specific answers then casting two creatures to trigger [**Vengevine**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/51eb9f05-9d5a-4196-9329-626ce4793c42?format=image)'s recursion to return to the battlefield with Haste
- [**Stoneforge Mystic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19557351-b65f-4b04-b971-66abdc07000a?format=image) tutors [**Basilisk Collar**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/55cdba1b-7a80-435f-9cff-b9365f62e311?format=image), which equips to [**Cunning Sparkmage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/699842c1-6507-48ee-b98b-3774d1f07c76?format=image) (haste pinger) with Deathtouch + Haste pinging creates a repeatable, instant-speed removal engine that kills any opposing creature each turn for free
- [**Fauna Shaman**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c685e4c3-eb7b-4b9e-9676-395d69d80974?format=image) acts as a tutor for singletons tailored to any matchup, [**Realm Razer**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/da3ecfc6-1f9e-443e-a445-51df518025a5?format=image) to exile all lands upon entering, acting as a complete mana-lockout against ramp/control decks, [**Linvala, Keeper of Silence**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/82b80a09-7e75-4091-a60e-04aff79339a3?format=image) to shut down activated abilities of opposing creatures (shutting down mirror matches), [**Obstinate Baloth**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6694496c-45b9-4ddf-bfcd-b632441b8811?format=image) or [**Baneslayer Angel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6764ea7b-ccb3-4f39-b8ba-654a186210b9?format=image) or [**Sun Titan**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d8db2b8e-dce9-49b7-833f-381ee55288cb?format=image) for high-impact threats for stabilization, value or game-ending lifegain
