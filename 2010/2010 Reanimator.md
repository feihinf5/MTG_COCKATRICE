Note
- Grand Prix Madrid 2010 winning deck by Andreas Muller
- [**Mystical Tutor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5d98101f-e32a-4a4a-a649-faa920d111ee?format=image) to tutor up instant-speed combo pieces ([**Entomb**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f60a2091-fb97-4f04-911b-fce9b6351044?format=image), [**Reanimate**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ae1ef31c-8ca5-444c-8f39-e1d1827318f5?format=image)) or countermagic protection ([**Force of Will**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9a879b60-4381-447d-8a5a-8e0b6a1d49ca?format=image)) at will
- Silver-Bullet Creature Toolbox, running singletons like [**Iona, Shield of Emeria**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/62458cfe-7231-4e09-b398-9c4d9330d08e?format=image) (locks opponent out of a chosen color), [**Blazing Archon**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cd0bc05b-ec48-41fd-a97a-ffb0d5a2dee0?format=image) (stops all attacks), and [**Inkwell Leviathan**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cb380a5a-a3f9-42fb-ac5c-f54afa3c1079?format=image) (unanswerable Shroud) allowed the deck to tutor up the exact target that instantly won against specific matchups
- [**Mystical Tutor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5d98101f-e32a-4a4a-a649-faa920d111ee?format=image) was banned in Legacy format during June 2010 https://web.archive.org/web/20160117184748/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/95b
