Note
- Grand-Prix Brussels 2010 winning deck by Emanuele Giusti
- Built around Cascade ([**Bloodbraid Elf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ef67487-c8e5-49bb-b0f7-e073ff2e31f1?format=image)) into devastating 3-mana spells like [**Blightning**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3c05e8a2-b7d0-4f24-b2ae-8e4db30e5842?format=image), [**Sprouting Thrinax**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8950df86-1e27-4b6b-8b5c-25298a3bda85?format=image) and [**Maelstrom Pulse**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/eb651c3a-cb27-4b73-8eb6-b87d65211097?format=image) to constantly generate free card advantage
- Features creature-lands ([**Raging Ravine**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cb7b6fb5-4d38-4734-b46f-923322cff24e?format=image), [**Lavaclaw Reaches**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c7066095-f05a-4f2e-ab9c-47c498608ccb?format=image)), allowing the deck to flood the board with win conditions even after running out of spells
- Runs a full set of [**Putrid Leech**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aaa47568-5668-4a9f-ad1c-9a13010ffc2b?format=image) alongside [**Garruk Wildspeaker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ca6f13a2-9243-4ce9-9f71-bed74355b781?format=image) instead of pure mana ramp, leaning harder into pressure and aggressive board presence
- Closes out games with high-impact threats ([**Siege-Gang Commander**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9f5128dd-54dd-42a8-b126-f821f87ad069?format=image), [**Broodmate Dragon**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aea1360e-7c6b-400c-893a-82d93e53101e?format=image)) that generate multiple bodies to stall or overwhelm opponents
