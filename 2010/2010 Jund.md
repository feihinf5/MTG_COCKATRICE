Note
- Grand-Prix Brussels 2010 winning deck by Emanuele Giusti
- Built around Cascade ([**Bloodbraid Elf**]) into devastating 3-mana spells like [**Blightning**], [**Sprouting Thrinax**] and [**Maelstrom Pulse**] to constantly generate free card advantage
- Features creature-lands ([**Raging Ravine**], [**Lavaclaw Reaches**]), allowing the deck to flood the board with win conditions even after running out of spells
- Runs a full set of [**Putrid Leech**] alongside [**Garruk Wildspeaker**] instead of pure mana ramp, leaning harder into pressure and aggressive board presence
- Closes out games with high-impact threats ([**Siege-Gang Commander**], [**Broodmate Dragon**]) that generate multiple bodies to stall or overwhelm opponents
