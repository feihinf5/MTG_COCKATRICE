Note
- World Championships 2010 Extended format top deck by Pascal Maynard
- [**Tempered Steel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6661b39d-505a-48f4-bc06-59084c6a3b0c?format=image) alongside [**Master of Etherium**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/322cdf67-5b36-43f9-99b3-f0e24d423314?format=image) and [**Steel Overseer**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b9da673d-7cc0-4435-b5a5-5098630f7712?format=image), allowing 0-cost creatures to instantly become massive threats
- Combines 0-mana artifacts ([**Memnite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/469cc4e0-49c0-4009-97ea-28e44addec69?format=image), [**Ornithopter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/22bb9825-ad45-44ad-ace2-eb6b66e3f724?format=image)) with [**Mox Opal**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6be9b1d5-9ab8-4adb-ba54-2c0117e842fa?format=image) and [**Springleaf Drum**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fa8b09d0-fbd2-4441-9d87-02450412e0db?format=image) to generate immediate mana velocity and turn 1–2 board presence
- [**Thoughtseize**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3df8c148-e87d-4043-9d8b-ec72bf8b6d5d?format=image) and [**Tidehollow Sculler**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1abecc77-07f2-43e4-8585-0a8199cdcf01?format=image) (an artifact creature itself) to strip key board wipes or combo pieces from the opponent before swinging in
- [**Ranger of Eos**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1a30ee26-5f78-4ac2-9105-1baa9ece8a21?format=image) to tutor up 0-cost and 1-cost creatures ([**Memnite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/469cc4e0-49c0-4009-97ea-28e44addec69?format=image), [**Ornithopter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/22bb9825-ad45-44ad-ace2-eb6b66e3f724?format=image), [**Court Homunculus**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fd20150c-ae49-457d-9f64-b72160745ff9?format=image)), instantly refueling the hand and board late in the game
- [**Thopter Foundry**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/42b8d797-b01d-49cf-9818-d84bba17029d?format=image) as an alternative win condition and life-gain engine, sacrificing redundant artifacts to build an evasive flying army.
