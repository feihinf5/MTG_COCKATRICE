Note
- Grand Prix-Oakland 2010 winning deck by Matt Nass
- Cast [**Primal Command**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/40d2c5b9-cef9-4763-8e65-e3b1418c0ad3?format=image) to put target non-creature permanent on top of its owner's library, search then cast [**Eternal Witness**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c7e10ca7-1e5d-4224-82cf-798a4d436d72?format=image) to returns [**Primal Command**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/40d2c5b9-cef9-4763-8e65-e3b1418c0ad3?format=image) from your graveyard to your hand, cast any creature to bounce [**Eternal Witness**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c7e10ca7-1e5d-4224-82cf-798a4d436d72?format=image) back to your hand using [**Cloudstone Curio**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/47cbda17-d368-4dc3-b41c-95b146468b44?format=image), repeat this process indefinitely to put every single land they control on top of their library
