Note
- Japan Legacy Championship 2010 winning deck by Nakatani Shinji
- [**Stoneforge Mystic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19557351-b65f-4b04-b971-66abdc07000a?format=image) to tutor for specific utility equipment, [**Basilisk Collar**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/55cdba1b-7a80-435f-9cff-b9365f62e311?format=image) (to ping creatures down or gain life in aggro mirrors) or [**Sword of Light and Shadow**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/61d08a91-665e-458c-9b8f-4ebd5f54a0d6?format=image)
