Note
- 2010 Japan National Championship winning deck by Mori Katsuhiro
- Fetchlands purely to thin the deck of land draws and guarantee landfall triggers for [**Searing Blaze**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/301f13dd-39b8-4a93-9c05-3dc4fa1f1c75?format=image)
- [**Teetering Peaks**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e56aca36-bb51-45e3-9ef9-9f9f2aa1e088?format=image) to pump hasty creatures
- Focuses heavily on high-power, temporary creatures ([**Ball Lightning**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3259a9d0-0add-4cf1-958c-9367c7441c62?format=image), [**Hellspark Elemental**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/256a861a-c488-4834-a419-5bd1eeba6873?format=image), [**Hell's Thunder**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/848f6c49-0913-4f12-886d-8b6defe8bfab?format=image)) to maximize immediate combat damage
- [**Kargan Dragonlord**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b669d2d9-649b-4941-9a76-9833bdf77ca4?format=image) provides an un-killable late-game threat that eats spare mana to grow into an evasive finisher
- [**Quenchable Fire**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ee1c0ded-2a80-4ed4-b9fc-3a18bf5c3239?format=image) and [**Earthquake**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19fb8ae1-a619-42bf-976f-2833777032d2?format=image) were maindecked specifically to combat the prominent Jund and U/W Control matchups of that era
