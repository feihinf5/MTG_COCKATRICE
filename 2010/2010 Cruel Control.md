Note
- World Championships 2010 Extended format top deck by Luis Scott-Vargas
- Uses [**Vivid Creek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2e3c2935-84ee-446d-b247-2f574ea84a8f?format=image), [**Vivid Meadow**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a535790b-d416-4807-bca0-acf6b192101c?format=image), [**Vivid Marsh**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/31f806c7-0063-4270-b37c-5363c41a7621?format=image) combined with [**Reflecting Pool**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3d03dc37-03fb-47b2-aa25-60a85d0d94dd?format=image) and filter lands ([**Mystic Gate**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3dfa866b-93e2-4365-91b0-f12d1f7c5395?format=image), [**Cascade Bluffs**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c3eede44-270a-481d-850b-b4862b9685ea?format=image), [**Sunken Ruins**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9d91a31c-b70a-45bd-a8dd-48d49b277f24?format=image)) to seamlessly fix mana across all five colors
- [**Cruel Ultimatum**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0d267804-23de-4521-ae64-4dd5cd836c6c?format=image) drains life, forces discards/sacrifices and returns a creature (like [**Wurmcoil Engine**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b9bdfa34-f608-40d4-b634-b260ad620f18?format=image)) from your graveyard
