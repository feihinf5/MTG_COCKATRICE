Note
- Grand Prix-Singapore 2011 winning deck by Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa
- [**Squadron Hawk**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/beddd409-0154-45a5-a20d-833cf1b5e1f4?format=image) tutors up to 3 extra copies on enter-the-battlefield, refilling your hand with cheap flying evasive bodies
- [**Stoneforge Mystic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19557351-b65f-4b04-b971-66abdc07000a?format=image) tutors and cheats out game-winning Equipment ([**Batterskull**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cd114ec3-d286-4c70-a122-3043bc53cc88?format=image), [**Sword of Feast and Famine**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/580b4818-2a01-46ad-b4d9-7d895a625bb3?format=image), [**Sword of War and Peace**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fab5bc6c-8943-4078-866a-5d02f9be0eef?format=image))
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) provides repeatable card filter, bounce protection and a game-ending ultimate threat
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) and [**Stoneforge Mystic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/19557351-b65f-4b04-b971-66abdc07000a?format=image) were banned in Standard format during July 2011 https://web.archive.org/web/20140622170236/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/feature/standard-bannings-explained-2011-06-20
