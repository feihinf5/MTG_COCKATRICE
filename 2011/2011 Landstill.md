Note
- 2011 Japan Legacy Championship winning deck by Yasuda Masayuki
- [**Standstill**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3ede3f6f-e642-4fe4-aa37-0f01cdf4d149?format=image) placed on the board when you have superior positioning or active [**Mishra's Factory**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a696c5b6-f216-454d-8029-74e84bbd1428?format=image), force the opponent to cast a spell (giving you 3 fresh cards) or slowly die to creature-land attacks
- [**Life from the Loam**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ac16d09-8bc7-407c-a757-666f4707bc90?format=image) recovers lands from the graveyard, creating an engine with [**Wasteland**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/99ff731b-8399-40c8-b539-ba6ba5783771?format=image) to repeatedly destroy opposing non-basic lands and recur destroyed [**Mishra's Factory**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a696c5b6-f216-454d-8029-74e84bbd1428?format=image)
- [**Mental Misstep**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/61e9c6df-1c84-4eab-9076-a4feb6347c10?format=image) counters crucial 1-drops for 0 mana
- [**Pernicious Deed**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ae4cbb33-4947-49f0-b612-a92141fbfaa6?format=image) wipe-all board cleaner that destroys low-cost non-land permanents while leaving your lands intact
- [**Jace, the Mind Sculptor**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0e606072-a3aa-4300-ba90-ec92a721fa76?format=image) dominates the mid-to-late game via card advantage, fate-sealing, or ultimate exile win
- [**Vedalken Shackles**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cef06f56-a7a8-4c0f-8755-56b46d2aa82c?format=image) & [**Repeal**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e7dd929-4bba-46a6-86c9-b8ed853eb721?format=image), steals or resets opposing threats that slipped past the counter-cards
