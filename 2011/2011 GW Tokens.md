Note
- Grand Prix-Hiroshima 2011 winning deck by Martin Juza
- Generates large token boards using [**Garruk Relentless**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b4160322-ff40-41a4-887a-73cd6b85ae45?format=image) (Wolf tokens), [**Elspeth Tirel**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ebe9116e-7b04-4f2a-aa67-89a42c6e1801?format=image) (1/1 Soldier tokens), [**Hero of Bladehold**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8a3853ec-e307-46e0-96d7-0706b5c45c5e?format=image) (attacking Soldier tokens), and [**Geist-Honored Monk**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5d51355e-55fa-43bb-a5de-fc55ac7b6446?format=image) (Spirit tokens)
- Closes out games out of nowhere with [**Overrun**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ae0559d4-0015-44e4-8ec4-08bb1c54eec5?format=image) (giving +3/+3 and trample to a wide token board) or by steadily pumping the entire team using [**Gavony Township**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b5f73443-2fe8-424f-8e71-fc7ce1f3a3eb?format=image) and [**Mikaeus, the Lunarch**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c22dc283-ea54-4344-b1ca-fd6cc05080d9?format=image)
- Uses 8 mana dorks ([**Birds of Paradise**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/307d4236-1e54-43e3-83f1-063d49d16dda?format=image), [**Avacyn's Pilgrim**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7eb39e97-53c2-4df0-9fb3-a3d6a24ec41f?format=image)) to regularly cast high-impact 3-drops on Turn 2, most notably [**Mirran Crusader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aaf7a821-3587-4aad-8411-fca5c96ab5c4?format=image) or [**Blade Splicer**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b8e56a28-713b-4a13-a601-1128cf117539?format=image)
- [**Mortarpod**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/fbd23da5-421f-41d0-bb60-59560da7dece?format=image) turns extra 1/1 tokens or Wolf tokens into repeatable pingers to remove small creatures or finish off low-life opponents, while [**Oblivion Ring**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9efc5a2a-eb76-410d-98e6-1455108faa52?format=image) provides flexible catch-all removal
