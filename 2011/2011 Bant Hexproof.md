Note
- The Finals11 Modern format 4-0 deck by Masuno Ryosuke
- Core threats like [**Geist of Saint Traft**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/35b57113-b39a-460b-b4aa-02606b40bbd0?format=image), [**Troll Ascetic**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f01233c2-6df0-4f0e-8668-3855868731ea?format=image) and [**Thrun, the Last Troll**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5d393da0-4cb6-4ae8-b747-8e6d0fa7f55a?format=image) feature Hexproof, rendering single-target removal spells like completely useless against them
- Stacks powerful Auras ([**Angelic Destiny**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a0cd7438-fde2-4e26-9c34-52c476a971e9?format=image)) and Planeswalkers ([**Elspeth, Knight-Errant**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/44c52e52-2b1c-4ca8-ab6d-20d97a342704?format=image)) onto untargetable threats, quickly creating flying, high-power attackers that opponents cannot easily interact with
- Uses [**Worship**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/006c18cf-b6d2-4fba-b9da-c9762fb3885b?format=image) combined with hard-to-kill hexproof/regenerating creatures creates an instant soft-lock where your life total cannot drop below 1
