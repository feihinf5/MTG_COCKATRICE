Note
- The Finals11 Modern format 4-0 deck by Imai Satoshi
- Built on the core Jund philosophy of trading cards 1-for-1 via hand disruption ([**Inquisition of Kozilek**], [**Thoughtseize**]) and removal, then winning through superior card quality
- Uses [**Punishing Fire**] + [**Grove of the Burnwillows**] to generate repeatable 2-damage removal, easily controlling opponent small-creature decks and planeswalkers in the mid-to-late game
- [**Bloodbraid Elf**] acts as a 2-for-1 card-advantage engine, guaranteeing a free target spell (like [**Tarmogoyf**] or [**Liliana of the Veil**]) whenever played
