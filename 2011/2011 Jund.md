Note
- The Finals11 Modern format 4-0 deck by Imai Satoshi
- Built on the core Jund philosophy of trading cards 1-for-1 via hand disruption ([**Inquisition of Kozilek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6a3ff5c3-0fdb-4d54-b4e5-ce7bad9953f0?format=image), [**Thoughtseize**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3df8c148-e87d-4043-9d8b-ec72bf8b6d5d?format=image)) and removal, then winning through superior card quality
- Uses [**Punishing Fire**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/56e76f1c-5a07-455a-a3df-4c45b5b25b82?format=image) + [**Grove of the Burnwillows**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e0b4d4b1-6e25-4c4b-a21a-1b7b1c1d6452?format=image) to generate repeatable 2-damage removal, easily controlling opponent small-creature decks and planeswalkers in the mid-to-late game
- [**Bloodbraid Elf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ef67487-c8e5-49bb-b0f7-e073ff2e31f1?format=image) acts as a 2-for-1 card-advantage engine, guaranteeing a free target spell (like [**Tarmogoyf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6876d9e-0908-43ac-8542-09c7aa02b5ba?format=image) or [**Liliana of the Veil**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ac506c17-adc8-49c6-9d8d-43db7cb1ec9d?format=image)) whenever played
