Note
- World Championships 2011 undefeated Modern deck by Tzu-ching Kuo
- Features [**Cranial Plating**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/90ea95d0-9f95-462b-a080-22a5da7b2e97?format=image) which equips to evasive flyers ([**Vault Skirge**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f254239c-c07a-4c41-98f7-8f4de539c73e?format=image), [**Blinkmoth Nexus**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/bf51c665-7823-4d6a-b1da-8c2d93dae10b?format=image), [**Inkmoth Nexus**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ec50c1c3-885e-47d3-ada7-cc0edbf09df1?format=image), [**Signal Pest**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/be065962-f2ed-4ab9-be6b-bfc66d63ff4e?format=image)) to deal massive damage based on total artifact count
- [**Atog**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/a15397bc-c400-4f8d-b206-08a4d65e76ff?format=image) and [**Arcbound Ravager**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/72c1a731-7854-42b1-8719-ac3c2a269c1f?format=image) feed on spare artifacts to instantly grow to lethal size
- Combines with [**Shrapnel Blast**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/056affab-4e2a-4b68-b864-d879becd3c45?format=image) (5 damage to the face) or [**Galvanic Blast**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f5881bbc-8600-464d-9dcd-5a7780918d1d?format=image) (4 damage for 1 mana with Metalcraft) to burn the opponent out from high life totals
