Note
- World Championships 2011 Modern format undefeated deck by Scott Richards
- Leverages cheap, undercosted threats, [**Delver of Secrets**] (flipping into a 3/2 flyer) and [**Tarmogoyf**] (scaling into a massive 4/5 or 5/6 body)—to quickly establish early pressure
- Integrates [**Spellstutter Sprite**] alongside [**Vendilion Clique**] to counter 1-drops or 2-drops while leaving behind a flying body
- Combines classic red removal ([**Lightning Bolt**], [**Flame Slash**], [**Burst Lightning**]) to keep opposing blockers clear, finish off low-life opponents or trigger Kicker on direct damage for late game
- Protects early threats using soft counters like [**Mana Leak**] and [**Deprive**] (returning a land to hand), scaling up to [**Cryptic Command**] to tap down blockers or counter/draw in top-deck wars
- [**Snapcaster Mage**] gives flashback to your burn, cantrips ([**Serum Visions**], [**Sleight of Hand**]) or soft counters, ensuring you never run out of gas while keeping up tempo
