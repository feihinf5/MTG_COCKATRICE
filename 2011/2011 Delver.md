Note
- World Championships 2011 Modern format undefeated deck by Scott Richards
- Leverages cheap, under costed threats, [**Delver of Secrets**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/11bf83bb-c95b-4b4f-9a56-ce7a1816307a?format=image) (flipping into a 3/2 flyer) and [**Tarmogoyf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6876d9e-0908-43ac-8542-09c7aa02b5ba?format=image) (scaling into a massive 4/5 or 5/6 body)—to quickly establish early pressure
- Integrates [**Spellstutter Sprite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4e5ba4a9-a282-4d4b-b25a-179e05e458f4?format=image) alongside [**Vendilion Clique**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f53d8540-fb6d-4d4c-b467-ebfbfa53c880?format=image) to counter 1-drops or 2-drops while leaving behind a flying body
- Combines classic red removal ([**Lightning Bolt**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/435589bb-27c6-4a6d-9d63-394d5092b9d8?format=image), [**Flame Slash**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/006d2bf1-20f7-4b09-8d98-8233d91682bd?format=image), [**Burst Lightning**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2dc16614-5cf8-444d-a5ae-cac25018af68?format=image)) to keep opposing blockers clear, finish off low-life opponents or trigger Kicker on direct damage for late game
- Protects early threats using soft counters like [**Mana Leak**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/56707af4-eb74-4b33-8741-6cc9b547d919?format=image) and [**Deprive**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2efecdd9-bd3a-4b79-92da-6485589d5bde?format=image) (returning a land to hand), scaling up to [**Cryptic Command**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/829e3d6e-5d7c-4cc4-a7a6-7cbf5a7442ba?format=image) to tap down blockers or counter/draw in top-deck wars
- [**Snapcaster Mage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/9e5b279e-4670-4a1e-87d0-3cab7e4f9e58?format=image) gives flashback to your burn, cantrips ([**Serum Visions**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/4bc61952-88ba-447a-835a-f1e9643fcd0d?format=image), [**Sleight of Hand**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d1b72631-d537-46e1-b9b3-618fba8a4c46?format=image)) or soft counters, ensuring you never run out of gas while keeping up tempo
