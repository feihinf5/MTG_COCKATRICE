Note
- World Championships 2011 winning deck by Andrew Roistan
- Pairs with [**Bloodbraid Elf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ef67487-c8e5-49bb-b0f7-e073ff2e31f1?format=image) cascading into [**Boom // Bust**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7ce986a4-5f82-4e7e-bef5-b49f05bf96a6?format=image) allowed players (under older rules) to choose to cast the Bust side for free, blowing up all lands on the board
- Casts [**Blood Moon**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2d75a5e3-4bec-4c2c-a9ad-f894cde292fd?format=image) as early as Turn 2 off [**Noble Hierarch**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/6adfe928-1305-444d-b709-1e714544daaf?format=image) to completely shut down greedy multi-color mana bases before they can react
- Features the premier creature package of the era, [**Wild Nacatl**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5eb18038-eb6a-4d27-ba52-52a1cee6b512?format=image) (3/3 for 1 mana), [**Tarmogoyf**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/b6876d9e-0908-43ac-8542-09c7aa02b5ba?format=image) and [**Knight of the Reliquary**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ad8b8518-c09e-4cb7-95b2-08e4e370d89c?format=image), which scales massively off your fetchlands and destroyed lands
- [**Knight of the Reliquary**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ad8b8518-c09e-4cb7-95b2-08e4e370d89c?format=image) tutors utility lands ([**Ghost Quarter**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/893eb7e4-5d8d-477b-aaa7-fb85ef2a54fc?format=image), [**Treetop Village**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/de15f8fd-499c-4fa0-9a10-425f81c61579?format=image)) while simultaneously thinning the deck and pumping its own stats
- [**Wild Nacatl**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5eb18038-eb6a-4d27-ba52-52a1cee6b512?format=image) was banned in Modern format during December 2011 https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/brief-history-modern-banned-list-2015-02-03
