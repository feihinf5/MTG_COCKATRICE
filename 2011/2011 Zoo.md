Note
- World Championships 2011 winning deck by Andrew Roistan
- Targets your own Fetchland or [**Ghost Quarter**] with Boom, you sacrifice/crack your fetchland in response to the spell, destroying your opponent's land for 2 mana with zero downside
- Pairs with [**Bloodbraid Elf**] cascading into [**Boom // Bust**] allowed players (under older rules) to choose to cast the Bust side for free, blowing up all lands on the board while keeping your superior creatures (Tarmogoyf, Knight of the Reliquary) intact.

Maindeck Blood Moon Lock: Casts Blood Moon as early as Turn 2 off Noble Hierarch to completely shut down greedy multi-color mana bases before they can react.

Aggressive Zoo Beaters: Features the premier creature package of the era—Wild Nacatl (3/3 for 1 mana), Tarmogoyf, and Knight of the Reliquary, which scales massively off your fetchlands and destroyed lands.

Land-Toolbox Synergy: Knight of the Reliquary tutors utility lands (Ghost Quarter, Treetop Village) while simultaneously thinning the deck and pumping its own stats.
