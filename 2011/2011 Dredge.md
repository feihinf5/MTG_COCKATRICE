Note
- 2011 Vintage Championship winning deck by Mark Hornung
- [**Bazaar of Baghdad**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ff37b863-f8c4-4584-8cc2-ac0e096e583f?format=image) draws 2 and discards 3 for 0 mana, you replace the draws with Dredge effects, milling 10–12+ cards per turn effortlessly
- [**Serum Powder**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/8330afd6-f43a-4955-a704-8f2b963cd0c6?format=image) used aggressively during mulligans to exile hands without [**Bazaar of Baghdad**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ff37b863-f8c4-4584-8cc2-ac0e096e583f?format=image), drastically increasing the statistical odds of starting with [**Bazaar of Baghdad**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ff37b863-f8c4-4584-8cc2-ac0e096e583f?format=image) on Turn 1
- The Dredgers ([**Golgari Grave-Troll**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f61b50e6-2166-435d-bf48-c4a0cff9999c?format=image), [**Stinkweed Imp**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/628903a0-6695-4643-80f3-9a6efc4d6a27?format=image), [**Golgari Thug**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/87c39e21-3e2f-4cbf-9f99-69e977924a73?format=image), [**Dakmor Salvage**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/72474b66-1dda-409d-8703-b026c1777081?format=image)), these cards replace your draws to quickly fill your graveyard
- Free Creature Recursion, [**Narcomoeba**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f76b3746-2e2c-4560-a2d2-e7b5b92833b2?format=image) enters the battlefield for free when milled from your library,  [**Bloodghast**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/63870c81-63bf-4a9a-aeb5-74c6eaded9f1?format=image) & [**Ichorid**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/97431dca-54ca-47ef-ab00-943140e8e758?format=image) trigger free recursion via land drops or exiling Black creatures from the graveyard,  these creatures serve as free fodder for [**Cabal Therapy**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0a5df970-c6ba-4824-b8ba-67244aec2b82?format=image) and [**Dread Return**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d7e304fc-0ace-459e-8d2f-376f1899639c?format=image)
- [**Bridge from Below**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/52c44610-6d4b-4c14-839f-2c085badec90?format=image) generates a horde of 2/2 Zombie tokens whenever your non-token creatures die
- [**Dread Return**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d7e304fc-0ace-459e-8d2f-376f1899639c?format=image) sacrifices 3 free creatures to reanimate a massive threat
- [**Flame-Kin Zealot**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/aec53f16-5bf7-4ef5-b83e-9e36463d1640?format=image) gives your entire army of Zombie tokens +1/+1 and Haste for an immediate One-Turn-Kill swing
- [**Sun Titan**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d8db2b8e-dce9-49b7-833f-381ee55288cb?format=image) acts as a utility finisher that can reanimate [**Bazaar of Baghdad**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ff37b863-f8c4-4584-8cc2-ac0e096e583f?format=image) or [**Fatestitcher**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7e4becff-8280-4e29-a370-bf82fb6734d5?format=image) directly from the graveyard
- [**Fatestitcher**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7e4becff-8280-4e29-a370-bf82fb6734d5?format=image) can be Unearthed from the graveyard for 1 Blue mana to untap [**Bazaar of Baghdad**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ff37b863-f8c4-4584-8cc2-ac0e096e583f?format=image), allowing you to dredge twice in a single turn
