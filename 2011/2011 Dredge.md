Note
- 2011 Vintage Championship winning deck by Mark Hornung
- [**Bazaar of Baghdad**] draws 2 and discards 3 for 0 mana, you replace the draws with Dredge effects, milling 10–12+ cards per turn effortlessly
- [**Serum Powder**] used aggressively during mulligans to exile hands without [**Bazaar of Baghdad**], drastically increasing the statistical odds of starting with [**Bazaar of Baghdad**] on Turn 1
- The Dredgers ([**Golgari Grave-Troll**], [**Stinkweed Imp**], [**Golgari Thug**], [**Dakmor Salvage**]), these cards replace your draws to quickly fill your graveyard
- Free Creature Recursion, [**Narcomoeba**] enters the battlefield for free when milled from your library,  [**Bloodghast**] & [**Ichorid**] trigger free recursion via land drops or exiling Black creatures from the graveyard,  these creatures serve as free fodder for [**Cabal Therapy**] and [**Dread Return**]
- [**Bridge from Below**] generates a horde of 2/2 Zombie tokens whenever your non-token creatures die
- [**Dread Return**] sacrifices 3 free creatures to reanimate a massive threat
- [**Flame-Kin Zealot**] gives your entire army of Zombie tokens +1/+1 and Haste for an immediate One-Turn-Kill swing
- [**Sun Titan**] acts as a utility finisher that can reanimate [**Bazaar of Baghdad**] or [**Fatestitcher**] directly from the graveyard
- [**Fatestitcher**] can be Unearthed from the graveyard for 1 Blue mana to untap [**Bazaar of Baghdad**], allowing you to dredge twice in a single turn
