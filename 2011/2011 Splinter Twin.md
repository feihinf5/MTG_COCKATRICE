Note
- Pro Tour Philadelphia 2011 winning deck by Samuele Estratti
- Wins instantly by casting [**Splinter Twin**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2f8f22fb-7291-4517-9b15-e98501f2856b?format=image) or [**Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/162018eb-5483-4fa2-9c5a-abb639eecf91?format=image) targeting [**Deceiver Exarch**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1f123ad6-fe84-4fed-9c0f-6b41921e9c26?format=image) or [**Pestermite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f252ae53-443c-4a27-b8f0-639a9a2b8598?format=image), untapping the creature on enter-the-battlefield to generate infinite haste tokens
- Uses flash creatures ([**Deceiver Exarch**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1f123ad6-fe84-4fed-9c0f-6b41921e9c26?format=image)/[**Pestermite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/f252ae53-443c-4a27-b8f0-639a9a2b8598?format=image)) to tap down opponent lands or threats at their end step, forcing them to tap out before the combo turn
- Runs [**Spellskite**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1a84bada-ed6a-4e97-8a0c-05b7cb32d66f?format=image) to absorb targeted removal or aggressive spells directed at the combo pieces
- [**Ponder**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ba6b6fc5-5077-4812-b8e9-906783dbaf67?format=image), [**Preordain**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e3868c3d-4fcd-444b-866f-0f8e50ce7b67?format=image), [**Sleight of Hand**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/d1b72631-d537-46e1-b9b3-618fba8a4c46?format=image), to dig rapidly for missing combo pieces or protection
- [**Firespout**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/13454f69-1458-4c03-ab02-bd697a32eb17?format=image) wipes aggressive creature boards while keeping 1/4 [**Deceiver Exarch**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/1f123ad6-fe84-4fed-9c0f-6b41921e9c26?format=image) alive
- [**Ponder**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ba6b6fc5-5077-4812-b8e9-906783dbaf67?format=image) and [**Preordain**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e3868c3d-4fcd-444b-866f-0f8e50ce7b67?format=image) were banned in Modern format since September 2011 https://web.archive.org/web/20221202053322/https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/explanation-september-2011-br-changes-2011-09-20-0
