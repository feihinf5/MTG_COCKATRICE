Note
- 2011 British National Championship winning deck by Daniel Royde
- [**Garruk Wildspeaker**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0135ada3-e5ae-4382-a717-872839bf1d5a?format=image), untaps lands ([**Eldrazi Temple**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cbab7e1f-305e-4733-aa70-b27285740925?format=image) / [**Eye of Ugin**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c3b21941-1b7d-4fde-8b1d-7edbd5e5b796?format=image)) to double mana production or generate 3/3 Beast blockers
- [**Green Sun's Zenith**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/02335747-54e3-4827-ae19-4e362863da9b?format=image) provides unmatched consistency—tutors
- [**Fauna Shaman**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/c685e4c3-eb7b-4b9e-9676-395d69d80974?format=image) discards excess mana dorks or high-cost Titans to fetch situational silver-bullets
- [**Primeval Titan**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/feee9327-b937-46ba-a2aa-6c015ab6cdd5?format=image), the central engine of the deck, on entry and attack, fetches key utility lands
- [**Emrakul, the Aeons Torn**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/67600383-bbb8-411c-b8e6-2296650bc747?format=image), the ultimate finisher, takes an extra turn on cast and annihilates the opponent's entire board upon attacking
- [**Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/225e3cc6-34d0-4f81-9f49-162d97e2ea59?format=image) & [**Artisan of Kozilek**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/3ac80eb8-321d-476a-87e7-d25bdac6a91c?format=image), destroys permanents on cast and reanimates creatures from the graveyard
- [**Terastodon**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e66d2f62-8a4a-4e8d-93e1-5dc802684106?format=image), destroys 3 non-creature permanents (planeswalkers, opposing equipment, or lands) instantly upon entry
- [**Beast Within**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/ce5b6d19-22e3-4f57-8f4d-a17e982286c7?format=image), instant-speed target removal for any problem permanent, including Planeswalkers
