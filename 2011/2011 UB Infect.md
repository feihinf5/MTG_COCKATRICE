Note
- The Finals11 winning deck by Okada Naoya
- Uses cantrips ([**Ponder**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/81c908ee-e70a-4406-a32d-ab5ab17e67b1?format=image), [**Gitaxian Probe**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/995486ce-58bb-4753-a812-0ca73ef1a235?format=image)) and self-mill ([**Forbidden Alchemy**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/eb22ae62-6207-4693-87cf-7adf0fc1fe29?format=image)) to rapidly stock the graveyard with instants and sorceries
- Equipping [**Runechanter's Pike**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/0f54e38b-b4a0-4406-a635-7a5ab3722f25?format=image) onto an unblockable [**Blighted Agent**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/cddaebde-a060-4510-8c97-68432d931987?format=image) or a resilient [**Phyrexian Crusader**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/32aaa8b9-987b-4809-8a54-aa29bdc18805?format=image) instantly turns small infect threats into one or two-shot lethal attackers
- Features [**Corrupted Conscience**](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/7276c584-76ed-4c20-a5ea-627c8f7751e6?format=image) to steal an opponent's high-power threat and grant it Infect for a surprise turnabout kill
